Elementals (the **"Game"**, "the app") is a monster-battling game for Android.
This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, what we do with it, how
long we keep it, and what you can tell us to do about it. It is written to be
read, not to be survived — if anything in it is unclear, write to us and we will
explain it.

**The short version.** To play, you need an account, so we store your email
address (or your Google account) and your game progress on our server. If you
buy something or watch a rewarded ad, we keep a record of it. Crash reports help
us fix bugs. Analytics and ads only run if you agree to them in the consent form
the app shows you. We do not sell your data, we show no ads unless you tap to
watch one, and you can delete your account from inside the app at any time.

## 2. What we collect, why, and on what legal basis

Everything we collect is listed below. Where a legal basis is named, it refers to
Article 6(1) of the GDPR (Regulation (EU) 2016/679).

### 2.1. Account data — to give you an account

Your email address and a hash of your password (we never store the password
itself), or — if you use Google Sign-In — your Google account identifier and the
email address on it; plus the username you choose.

**Purpose:** creating your account, signing you in, recovering access, and
contacting you about your account.
**Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(b) — performance of the contract you enter into by
accepting the [Terms of Service](terms-of-service.html).

### 2.2. Gameplay data — to run the game

Your pets and their nicknames, levels and stats; fights and their outcomes;
breeding history; in-game currency; quests and daily-reward streaks; your
activity feed; zone progress.

The Game is server-authoritative, which means this data has to live on our
server: it is the game state, not a copy of it.

**Purpose:** providing the Game.
**Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(b) — performance of the contract.

### 2.3. Purchase records — to deliver what you bought, and to keep books

For each purchase made through Google Play Billing: the Google Play order
identifier, a reference to the purchase token, the product bought, and the
status of the purchase (verified, refunded, revoked). We never see or store your
card number, bank details or billing address — those stay with Google.

**Purpose:** verifying the purchase server-side, granting the item, handling
refunds and chargebacks, and keeping the accounting records we are obliged to
keep.
**Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(b) — performance of the contract; and Art. 6(1)(c) —
compliance with a legal obligation (Polish tax and accounting law).

### 2.4. Rewarded-ad grants — to pay out the reward once

For each rewarded ad you choose to watch: the AdMob transaction identifier, the
ad placement, the reward amount and the time it was granted.

**Purpose:** making sure the reward is granted exactly once, enforcing the daily
cap, and detecting attempts to farm rewards fraudulently.
**Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(b) — performance of the contract (we owe you the
reward); and Art. 6(1)(f) — our legitimate interest in preventing fraud and
abuse of the reward system.

### 2.5. Crash reports and device information — to fix what breaks

When the app crashes, Google Firebase Crashlytics sends us a crash report: the
stack trace, the device model, the Android version, the app version, the amount
of free memory and similar technical state, together with a Crashlytics
installation identifier.

**Purpose:** finding and fixing crashes and bugs.
**Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(f) — our legitimate interest in keeping the Game
working. We have weighed this against your interests: a crash report describes
what the software did, not what you did, and it is not used to build a profile
of you or to target you with anything. You can object to this processing
(section 7).

### 2.6. Usage analytics — to see which parts of the Game are used

Google Firebase Analytics records events such as signing up, starting and
finishing a fight, breeding, and completing a purchase, together with a
pseudonymous app-instance identifier and coarse technical context (device model,
OS version, country). **No names and no email addresses are sent to Analytics.**

**Purpose:** understanding which features are used and where players get stuck,
so we can improve the Game.
**Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(a) — your consent. Analytics is collected **only
after** the in-app consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) records that
analytics may run. If you decline, analytics is not collected. You can change
your mind at any time (section 7).

### 2.7. Advertising — only the ads you ask for

The Game shows **rewarded ads only**, and only when you explicitly tap to watch
one in exchange for an in-game reward. No banners, no interstitials, nothing
that interrupts play. Every reward available through an ad is also reachable
without watching one.

When you tap to watch, Google AdMob serves the ad and may process your device's
**advertising ID**, device and network information, and your interaction with
the ad, in order to select, deliver, cap and measure it. In the EEA, the UK and
Switzerland, **personalised** ads are shown only if you agreed to them in the
consent form; if you did not, you get non-personalised ads, which still use
limited data to serve and measure the ad and to prevent fraud.

**Purpose:** delivering and measuring the rewarded ad you asked for, and
financing the Game.
**Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(a) — your consent, given in the consent form and
withdrawable at any time (section 7).

See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and
[AdMob's data disclosure](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543) for
what Google does as part of serving ads.

### 2.8. Your age bracket — to keep advertising age-appropriate

At sign-up we ask which age bracket you are in: **Under 13**, **13–17**, or
**18+**. **Under 13** cannot create an account. The answer is stored **on your
account**, alongside your username and email address.

**Purpose:** tagging ad requests as directed to a minor where that applies, so
that Google serves only ads suitable for a teenager. Keeping the answer on the
account rather than on one phone is what makes that tagging survive a reinstall
or a move to a second device.

**Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(f) — our legitimate interest in age-appropriate
advertising and in protecting minors. That interest is what the Google Play
Families policy and the AdMob programme policies require of us as the app's
publisher, so the same processing also serves a legal obligation under
Art. 6(1)(c).

How we handle it:

- it is shown only to you, on your own profile — never to another player;
- it is part of the copy of your data you can export (section 7);
- it is erased with your account (section 8), like your username and address;
- it can only be made **more** restrictive, never less: an account marked
  **13–17** cannot be moved to **18+** from the app;
- once your account carries an answer, we do not ask again: a reinstall or a
  second device reuses it. If your account was created before we started
  asking, the app asks you once per installation after you sign in — and you
  can decline; declining is remembered on that device only.

## 3. Do you have to give us this data?

3.1. **Account data and gameplay data: yes, if you want to play.** Providing them
is a contractual requirement. Without an email address (or a Google account) we
cannot create an account, and without an account the Game does not work — it has
no offline mode.

3.2. **Purchase records: yes, if you buy something.** We are legally required to
keep them once a purchase happens.

3.3. **Analytics and advertising: no.** Both are optional and consent-based. If
you decline them the Game works exactly the same; you simply cannot earn the
optional ad rewards, because there is no ad to watch.

3.4. **Crash reports:** collected on our legitimate interest, and you can object
(section 7).

## 4. What we do not do

- **We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for
  cross-context behavioural advertising**, as those terms are used in US state
  privacy laws such as the California CPRA. Nobody pays us for your data. The
  only money involved is the ad revenue Google pays us for ads you chose to
  watch, and Google's own use of the advertising ID for that is described in
  section 2.7 and happens, in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, only with your
  consent.
- **We do not send push notifications.** The app requests no notification
  permission and we run no messaging campaigns. If that ever changes, we will
  update this policy and ask for consent first.
- **We make no automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant
  effects about you, and we do not profile you** within the meaning of
  Art. 22 GDPR. Nothing in the Game decides anything about you outside the Game.
- **We do not use your data to train machine-learning models.**
- **We do not collect your precise location, your contacts, your photos, your
  microphone or your camera.** The app asks for none of those permissions.

## 6. How long we keep it

6.1. We keep data only as long as the purpose it was collected for lasts. A job
runs every night at 03:00 and deletes whatever has passed its window.

| Data | Kept for |
|---|---|
| Account (username, email, credentials) | Until you delete your account, or until **730 days** without signing in — then anonymised automatically |
| Pets, nicknames, gameplay state | While the account exists; nicknames are cleared on deletion |
| Fight records | **730 days** after the fight ended (an opponent's history references the same fight) |
| Activity feed | **180 days**; deleted immediately for you when you delete your account |
| Purchase ledger, rewarded-ad grants, daily-reward claims | **5 years** (1825 days) — the evidence we need for a tax enquiry or a chargeback |
| Daily quest claims | Cleared nightly at the quest reset |
| Admin moderation and audit log | **1 year** (365 days) |
| Sign-in (refresh) tokens | Deleted when you log out, when you delete your account, and immediately if a token is reused |
| Delivery records for account emails (verification, password reset, change of address) | **7 days** after sending; **30 days** for a send that failed, so a broken relay is still diagnosable; removed with your account |
| Crash reports | Per Google's Crashlytics retention — currently **90 days** |
| Analytics events | Per Google's Firebase Analytics retention settings |

6.2. **The event history.** Under the hood, the Game records every action as an
append-only event log — that log *is* the state of the game, and it is never
rewritten. Deleting your account therefore does not erase the historical events;
instead it **pseudonymises** them: your name, email and credentials are replaced
everywhere they are readable, so what remains is a history keyed to a random
identifier that no longer resolves to a person. A username you used in the past
may still appear inside an old event record. We treat this as a proportionate
application of Art. 17(3) GDPR — erasure that would require destroying the
integrity of the record itself — and it is why we describe the outcome as
anonymisation of your identity rather than deletion of every byte.

6.3. The purchase and reward ledgers deliberately outlive the account, for the
tax and chargeback reasons in section 2.3. Once your account is anonymised they
no longer identify you.

## 7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the following rights. All of them are free, and we
answer within one month.

- **Access** (Art. 15) — ask what we hold about you and get a copy. Write to
  krzysztof.gruszczynski.kg@gmail.com and we send it.
- **Rectification** (Art. 16) — have wrong or incomplete data corrected. You can
  change your username and pet nicknames yourself in the app.
- **Erasure** (Art. 17) — have your data deleted; see section 8, and see 6.2 for
  what happens to the event history.
- **Restriction** (Art. 18) — have us pause processing while a dispute about
  accuracy or lawfulness is resolved.
- **Portability** (Art. 20) — get the data you gave us in a machine-readable
  form. Open **Export my data** in your Profile in the app: it prepares a JSON
  file with your account data and hands it to the share sheet, so you can save
  or send it wherever you like. You can also ask us by email and we send the
  same file.
- **Objection** (Art. 21) — object to processing based on our legitimate
  interest, which here means crash reporting, the fraud checks on ad rewards,
  and the age bracket held for ad tagging (section 2.8).
  Tell us why and we will stop unless we have compelling grounds that override
  your interests.
- **Withdraw consent** (Art. 7(3)) — for analytics and ads, at any time, without
  affecting what was lawful before you withdrew it. Open **Ad privacy settings**
  in the app to reopen the consent form and change or revoke your answers. You
  can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android's own settings
  (Settings → Google → Ads).

**Complaints.** If you think we have handled your data unlawfully you can
complain to the Polish supervisory authority:

> ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, Poland

You may also complain to the supervisory authority of the EU country where you

To exercise any right, write to krzysztof.gruszczynski.kg@gmail.com. We may ask you to confirm you

## 8. Delete your account

8.1. **In the app:** open **Profile → Delete Account** and confirm. The deletion
runs immediately; no waiting period, no email exchange, no need to contact us.

8.2. **By email:** write to krzysztof.gruszczynski.kg@gmail.com from the address on your account
and ask us to delete it. We will do it and confirm.

8.3. **What is deleted:**

- your username and email address, in both your profile and your credentials,
  are replaced with a meaningless placeholder;
- your password hash is blanked and any link to your Google account is severed;
- every sign-in token you hold is invalidated and the token rows are deleted, so
  the account cannot be signed into again;
- every nickname you gave a pet is cleared;
- the age bracket you answered (section 2.8) is cleared;
- your whole activity history is deleted;
- the delivery records of every account email we sent you — verification,
  password reset, change of address — are deleted, including any that had not
  been sent yet, so nothing goes out to the address after you have left.

8.4. **What is kept, and why:**

- **Purchase records, rewarded-ad grants and daily-reward claims**, for 5 years,
  as evidence for tax enquiries and chargebacks. They are keyed to the
  anonymised account and no longer identify you.
- **Fight records**, for 730 days after the fight, because they are also part of
  your opponents' history.
- **Pets and game state**, in anonymised form, because the fight history refers
  to them. They carry no name or free text once nicknames are cleared.
- **The append-only event history**, pseudonymised as described in section 6.2.

8.5. The same erasure runs automatically on any account left unused for **730
days**, on exactly the same code path.

8.6. Deleting your account does not refund purchases. Refunds are handled by
Google Play — see section 9 of the [Terms of Service](terms-of-service.html).

9.1. The Game is **not directed at children under 13** and we do not knowingly

9.2. Users aged **13 to 17** may use the Game only with the consent of a parent
or guardian, who must accept the Terms of Service on their behalf — the same

9.3. At sign-up the app asks which age bracket you are in (section 2.8), and the
answer is kept on your account. If you answer **13–17**, ad requests are tagged
as directed to a minor so that only age-appropriate, non-personalised
advertising is served — on every device you sign in on, not only the one you

9.4. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 has created an
account, write to krzysztof.gruszczynski.kg@gmail.com and we will delete it.

## 10. How we protect your data

- All traffic between the app and our server runs over **TLS** (HTTPS/WSS).
- Passwords are stored only as a **hash** — we cannot read them, and a copy of
  the database would not reveal them.
- Sign-in tokens are held in **encrypted storage on your device**, not in plain
  files.
- Access to the production database and server is limited to the developer, over
  authenticated connections.
- Deleting an account invalidates every token already issued for it, so old
  sessions cannot survive the deletion.

No system is perfect. If a breach ever puts your rights at risk, we will notify
the supervisory authority within 72 hours and tell you directly where the law
requires it.
